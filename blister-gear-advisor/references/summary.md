## 2015–16 (first edition) highlights
- Ski classes: All-Mountain, More Forgiving, Chargers, Freestyle Narrower/Wider, Park, Frontside, Women's, Powder Directional/Playful, 50/50, Touring.
- Best Of skis include Fischer Motive 86Ti, Nordica Enforcer, Armada AR7, Nordica Soul Rider, Line Sir Francis Bacon, Liberty Variant 113, Moment Bibby Pro, ON3P Jeffrey 114, Line Pandora 110, G3 Zenoxide 105, Volkl V-Werks BMT 109, Blizzard Zero G 108. Salomon MTN Lab boot named product of the year.
- Only Line Sick Day reviewed: Sick Day 110 (50/50; same ski as women's Pandora 110).
- Gap: no guides 2016–17 to 2021–22 in the data.

## Constant across all six
- No ad money from reviewed manufacturers; no paid awards; no ski MSRPs; multi-day testing; "think before you buy."
- Mass ↔ stability: Blister consistently pushes back on ever-lighter skis while praising light skis that punch above their weight.
- Categories blurring; many skis listed in 2–3 sections; Spectrums are relative within a class.

## Line overhauls by year
- **2022–23:** K2 Dispatch skis/boots; Line Blade Optic; Nordica Unleashed (replaces Soul Rider); Atomic Bent 110; Faction renames (Dancer, Mana, La Machine); Fischer Ranger; Dynastar M-Pro 108; Salomon S/Pro Alpha boots. Light versions of favorites (Hustle 10/11, Unlimited 93/104).
- **2023–24:** Blizzard Rustler/Sheeva; Salomon Stance; Volkl Secret 102; DPS Kaizen, Koala 111; Line Bacon & Chronic; Head Oblivion; Moment Countach 110. BOA boots spread; Lange Shadow.
- **2024–25:** Blizzard Anomaly (replaces Bonafide/Brahma/Cochise) and Black Pearl refresh; Nordica Enforcer & Santa Ana; Völkl M7 Mantra, Blaze, Peregrine; Line Optic/Vision/Pandora; Icelantic Nomad/Maiden; K2 Reckoner/Omen; DPS Wailer returns; Faction Mana → Studio; Rossignol Sender Free 118 (≈ Blackops 118). Shift2.
- **2025–26:** Völkl renames all metal all-mountain skis "Mantra"; Head Kore → heavier, damper Kore Ti; Salomon QST 94/100; Dynastar M-Free 100. Dual-BOA/buckle-less boots; Tyrolia Attack Hybrid.
- **2026–27:** K2 Mindbender overhaul with targeted mass weighting; Blizzard Canvas; Icelantic Torrent/Tempest; Armada AntiMatter; Faction Dancer (Ti / non-Ti); Ferreol "Scalium" alloy. Lange Code 140 new AT downhill benchmark; easier-to-rip skin glues.

## Recurring benchmarks / repeat Best Of picks
K2 Mindbender 99Ti (do-it-all; succeeded by Mindbender 96 in 26–27), Moment Wildcat, Atomic Bent Chetler 120, Rossignol Blackops 118 → Sender Free 118, Folsom Rapture, Majesty Superwolf, 4FRNT Raven, Line Blade/Optic, Armada Edollo, Tecnica Zero G Tour Pro (touring boot reference), Scarpa F1 LT (being phased out by 26–27), ATK Raider (touring binding benchmark every year), Spark R&D (splitboard bindings), ZipFit liners.

## Recurring sub-themes
Sustainability (WNDR algae-based materials, flax, recycled stringers, G3 R3 recyclable skis, Fischer Curv GT Redefine, PFC/PFAS-free Gore-Tex); fun short-radius carvers (Line Blade, Black Crows Mirus Cor, Season Kin, Folsom Spar Turbo); small/custom builders (Folsom, Wagner, Parlor, Prior); hybrid touring bindings maturing to alpine-level downhill; volume-shifted / swallowtail pow snowboards and fast-entry bindings; magnetic and photochromic goggles; helmet safety framed by Ellsworth's broken-neck story every year.
